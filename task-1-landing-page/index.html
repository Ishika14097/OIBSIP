Our studio story

Started with a borrowed camera in a college dorm room, not a business plan.

Lumière began when our founder started shooting friends' portraits between classes, just to learn how light actually behaved. What came out of those first sessions looked nothing like the stiff studio shots everyone was used to — so she kept going.

Today we work with couples, brands, and individuals who want the same thing those first clients did: photos that feel like them, not like everyone else's gallery.
